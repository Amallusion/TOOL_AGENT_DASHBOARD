      What this number really measures

      It isn’t a count of who exists. It’s a count of what the web says out loud. Founders’ stories rarely make it into press releases, so the agent marks diversity as unknown rather than guessing.

      This is exactly where a community knows more than any search engine. Every founder who adds their story, and every reviewer who confirms what the agent couldn’t, makes the map fairer.

      How the agent finds them

      A search-and-read agent that does the legwork, and a person who has the final say.

      1. 1

        Spots the gaps

        It looks at what is already mapped in a category and works out which materials, places or approaches are missing.

      2. 2

        Reads the source

        It searches the web and reads each organisation’s own pages, not just the headlines written about them.

      3. 3

        Flags what’s unsure

        Anything it can’t confirm is marked uncertain, and nothing joins the guide until a person has checked it.

      The rules it follows

      • Facts come from real sources it has read, never from the model’s memory alone.
      • When something isn’t supported, it says “uncertain” instead of filling the gap.
      • Every finding carries its sources, so a reviewer can check it in seconds.
      • Organisations linked to human-rights or environmental harm are flagged for review, not silently removed.
